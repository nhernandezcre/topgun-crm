## App Store Connect description

**Title**
Should I Buy This?

**Subtitle (30 char)**
One verdict. One decision.

**Promotional text**
Before you tap buy, tap this. Real prices, real Reddit reviews, one honest answer.

**Description**
Your friend who's really into Reddit, in app form. We check prices, read the reviews you won't, and tell you straight up if you should buy it. No fluff, no affiliate-bait rankings, no top-10 listicles. One verdict. One decision.

Point your phone at anything. Paste any product link. Drop a screenshot from your cart. The app returns one of three answers in 8 seconds: BUY, SKIP, or WAIT. Behind that verdict is the real price (median across every retailer we could find), a price history chart for Amazon items, a fake-sale flag, a two-sentence read on what real owners are saying, and three specific cheaper alternatives with prices.

Features
- BUY / SKIP / WAIT verdicts with a 0-100 confidence score and a one-line reason
- Real market prices, not just retailer-listed
- Amazon price history with fake-sale detection
- Reddit synthesis, pulled from actual threads
- Three specific alternatives with names, prices, and why each is better
- Wishlist with push alerts when anything hits your target price
- Receipt: a running total of how much you've saved by listening to us
- Streaks to keep you checking before buying
- Shareable verdict cards designed for screenshots

What it's not
- Not a cashback app
- Not a price tracker with ten toolbars
- Not a listicle farm
- Not affiliate links disguised as reviews

$29/year. Less than one impulse purchase.
